Guide

How this works, and how to get through the hard days.

You are not behind.

You are about to do something most people talk about and never start: pick two numbers, tell the truth about them every night, and let 250 honest days do the rest.

You do not need a perfect run. You need a long one.

How to use this, in four steps

The scoring, plainly

Every 5 days you log — good day or bad day — one more state joins the Union. On day 250, the run is complete.

Why any of this matters

Twelve moments worth keeping in mind on a hard night. None of them happened because somebody felt like it.

Questions people ask

If things feel heavier than a habit

If you're struggling with your mood, feeling hopeless, or thinking about hurting yourself, please talk to someone today — a doctor, a counsellor, or someone you trust. In the US you can call or text 988 at any hour. This app is a habit tracker; it is not a substitute for real support, and reaching for that support is a strong move, not a weak one.
